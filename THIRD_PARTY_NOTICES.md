## Apps in Toss Examples

`toss-mcp` retrieves a selected, text-only snapshot from the official
[`toss/apps-in-toss-examples`](https://github.com/toss/apps-in-toss-examples)
repository at runtime. The selected files are indexed and chunked for search,
and every result retains its source path, commit, and URL.

its text in the validated local snapshot. The MIT license in this repository
